# Code of Conduct

We want bookdoe to be a friendly place to work on and talk about.

## Our standard

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- No publishing other people's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break
these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, follow the
private reporting steps in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and mark it as a conduct report.
